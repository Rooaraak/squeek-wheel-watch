How the wheel mines

01

The hamster runs

A hardware sensor on the wheel measures every turn: speed, rpm and distance, reported live.

02

Runs become work

Each round, Squeekchain's oracle closes the wheel's work and settles it on Sepolia as completed blocks.

03

Blocks mint SQK

Every block issues SQK on a halving schedule, with a launch bonus at the start, toward a 2.1 billion cap.
