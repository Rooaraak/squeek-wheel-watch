How a painting is made

Nothing here is drawn by hand or chosen by us. The hamster's numbers decide everything.

01

A real wheel

A sensor on Squeektoshi's wheel counts every turn (about 88 cm) and signs each reading on the device. Squeekchain turns that work into blocks.

02

Half-hour counts

Squeekchain publishes how many turns he made in every half hour of the Squeek day, 12:00 to 12:00 UTC. That list of 48 numbers is the recipe.

03

Paint on a clock

Each half hour he ran drops paint at that hour on a 12-hour face. More turns means a faster spin and longer trails. Colour runs from dusk to dawn, and gold marks a record: a new top speed or another 50 km.

04

Same numbers, same painting

Every random choice comes from a SHA-256 hash of those numbers, so anyone can re-create any painting from the public data with art.js.
