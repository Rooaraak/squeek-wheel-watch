How the wheel mines

01

The hamster runs

A Hall-effect sensor on an RP2040 board counts every turn of the wheel (about 88 cm of running each) and reports speed, rpm and distance.

02

Signed at the source

Each reading is signed on the device by a secure chip (Trust M, ECDSA P-256) before the oracle accepts it.

03

Runs become blocks

Every 10 minutes the oracle closes a round of the wheel's work and settles it on Sepolia as completed blocks.

04

Blocks mint SQK

Each block issues SQK on a halving schedule, plus a launch bonus for the first 97,020 blocks, toward a 2.1 billion cap.
